The Sun sign is the most common entry point into Western astrology: by the moment you were born, the Sun was in one of twelve signs, sketching the side of you that faces outward. Here is the basic dossier for all twelve.

| Pisces | ♓ | 2/19–3/20 | Water | Neptune |

## How to read the table

- **Element** tells how you meet the world: Fire is active and warm, Earth steady and practical, Air mental and communicative, Water feeling and intuitive.
- **Ruler** is the "director behind the scenes": Mars drives Aries, Venus grants Taurus and Libra their aesthetic sense, Mercury gives Gemini and Virgo their quickness.
- The Sun sign is the lead character, not the whole cast. A real chart also has the Moon (emotion), Rising (mask), Mercury (speech) and dozens more — so two people of the same sign can differ a lot.
