Western astrology sorts the 12 signs into **four elements**: Fire, Earth, Air, Water. They sit deeper than "which sign you are" — the element describes how your energy moves.

## Four energy portraits

- **Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)**: igniting. Act before thinking; warm, direct, loves the spotlight. Down side: burns out fast.
- **Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)**: grounding. Slow, practical, cares about results and texture. Down side: can be stubborn or fussy.
- **Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)**: flowing. Driven by thought, words and connection; full of ideas, socially wide. Down side: floats, hard to land.
- **Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)**: soaking. Reads the world through feeling and intuition; empathic, nostalgic. Down side: easy to brood.

## How elements get along

This is also the most-used "undercoat logic" in synastry scoring:

- **Same element**: in sync, high rapport — but can also amplify the same flaw (two Fires argue, two Earths go quiet).
- **Air feeds Fire, Earth nourishes Water**: traditionally the "mutually supportive" pairs — air gives fire oxygen, water gives earth moisture.
- **Fire & Water, Air & Earth**: more tension — one wants to burn, the other to stay still; needs more understanding.
