The last post covered the Stems & Branches alphabet. This one shows how they assemble into a **Bazi chart**.

## The Four Pillars

Bazi = the four groups of stems-and-branches at your birth moment — year, month, day, hour. Each group is one stem + one branch, eight characters in total, hence "Eight Characters" (八字).

- **Year pillar**: the year changes at Start-of-Spring, marked by the Gregorian year's stem-branch (e.g. 2026 Bing-Wu).
- **Month pillar**: changes at the solar terms, not on the 1st of each Gregorian month.
- **Day pillar**: the most important "self", derived from the exact date.
- **Hour pillar**: by the birth two-hour period (Zi 23–1, then sequentially).

## The Ten Gods: translating relations into plain words

Taking the day stem (representing "me") as the base, the five-element giving/receiving relationships between it and the other seven characters are summarized into ten "roles" — the **Ten Gods**:

- **Friend / Rob Wealth**: same as me — like siblings, peers.
- **Eating God / Hurt Officer**: what I output — talent, expression, children.
- **Direct Wealth / Indirect Wealth**: what I control — resources, money, the object of mastery.
- **Direct Officer / Seven Killings**: what controls me — rules, pressure, bosses.
- **Direct Seal / Indirect Seal**: what nurtures me — shelter, learning, the maternal.

<div class="warnbox">

**Heads up**: a real chart must account for solar terms, time zones and true solar time — easy to get wrong by hand. The site's **Birth Card** only gives the **year pillar** (Gregorian-year approximation) on purpose, to avoid handing you a chart that looks precise but may be off.

</div>

> ⚠️ The Ten Gods are a "role map" for self-reflection — fun as a mirror; as a verdict on fate, not so much.
