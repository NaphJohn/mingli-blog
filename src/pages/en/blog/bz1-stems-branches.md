Bazi (the Four Pillars) is the core framework of Chinese astrology, and its "alphabet" is the **Heavenly Stems** and **Earthly Branches**.

## The ten Stems

Jia, Yi, Bing, Ding, Wu, Ji, Geng, Xin, Ren, Gui.

Ancient thinkers imaged them as a tree's life cycle: Jia breaks ground, Yi sprouts branches, Bing-Ding flourish, Wu-Ji stabilize, Geng-Xin withdraw, Ren-Gui close and store. Mapped to elements: Jia-Yi Wood, Bing-Ding Fire, Wu-Ji Earth, Geng-Xin Metal, Ren-Gui Water.

## The twelve Branches

Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei, Shen, You, Xu, Hai.

Branches pair one-to-one with the twelve animals: Zi Rat, Chou Ox, Yin Tiger, Mao Rabbit, Chen Dragon, Si Snake, Wu Horse, Wei Goat, Shen Monkey, You Rooster, Xu Dog, Hai Pig. In time they mark the twelve months and the twelve two-hour periods of a day.

## The sexagenary cycle

Stems (10) and Branches (12) have LCM 60 — paired without repetition, they roll through a full circle: Jia-Zi, Yi-Chou, Bing-Yin … up to Gui-Hai. This is the **sixty-year cycle** used to mark year, month, day and hour.
